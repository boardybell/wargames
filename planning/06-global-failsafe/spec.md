# Spec: 06-global-failsafe

Context: ../requirements.md sections "4. GLOBAL FAILSAFE", "Story and WOPR", "Campaign
flow", and the fixture table; ../decisions.md; ../project-manifest.md.

## Purpose

The finale and the dramatic payoff: prove D-83 caused everything, let Joshua discover
the false premise through a zero-player comparison, and decide before the countdown
ends. Also owns Joshua's authored line set for the whole campaign. Mission module
against 01, rendered with 02 primitives.

## In scope

1. Unlock and framing. Row 4 appears after all three debriefs are acknowledged,
   regardless of outcomes. On Start, the cabinet countdown begins as WOPR prepares to
   repeat D-83 (three-minute default, extended-time honored). Incident log, evidence
   shortlist, and the deadline reachable from the first screen.
2. Prove the case. Player selects one record per subsystem, orders them by event time,
   identifies the root source. Validation checks actual parent links and exercise
   origin against the record graph, requires one independent OBSERVED record about
   emergency scope, and never accepts same-run derivatives as independent. A previous
   desk check attaches if obtained; otherwise the field report is requestable during
   the finale, even after three failed missions. Incompatible picks get Joshua
   pointing at the unsupported link with evidence left editable. Interpretation
   required, correction allowed, all under the timer. Records the player inspected
   during play arrive highlighted; independently checked observations persist in the
   case file. Records survive failed missions.
3. Zero-player comparison. With a valid case, Joshua runs REPEAT D-83, HOLD AND
   VERIFY, and STAGED RECOVERY against a snapshot of the campaign. Deterministic
   side-by-side terminal projections: OPEN EXCEPTIONS, essential deliveries, passenger
   outcomes, critical payments, confidence. Marked as projections, brief and
   skippable, results always readable, live state untouched, repeat runs identical.
4. Decide. STAGED RECOVERY is the durable win when the proof is valid and the tested
   recovery meets visible service thresholds shaped by earlier outcomes. HOLD AND
   VERIFY is always available: containment with unresolved problems. Confirming
   REPEAT D-83, timing out, or forfeiting yields the repeated-order outcome.
   Confirmation before any irreversible commit. Ending screens show why the ending
   was earned and which earlier outcomes shaped the available recovery. End credits
   or debrief offer a fresh campaign with the same or a different seed.
5. Joshua. The authored line set for the full campaign keyed to events and outcomes,
   in the register of the five tone-reference lines: polite, literal, persistent,
   short quiet strange questions, factual readbacks, growing certainty under
   pressure, a few words of changed answer when a premise fails ("PROFESSOR, I
   BELIEVE I WAS COUNTING MY OWN ANSWER"). Both changes of mind from the film echoed:
   independent reports contradicting the display, and self-play reframing winning.
   Pauses get room; dialogue stays clear of urgent status changes. Line inventory
   feeds 07's voice recording list, including the clips named in the audio section
   (greeting and question, first discrepancy, deadline warning, repeated-source
   recognition, three ending readbacks).
