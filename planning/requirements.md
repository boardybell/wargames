## Campaign flow

- Any of the three original choices can be the player's first mission. A completed, timed-out, or explicitly forfeited mission settles once and its result is final for that campaign. Save it as soon as the mission settles. The debrief presents the outcome, the effects that carry forward, and any clue found. Acknowledge the debrief to return to the menu. Selecting a settled mission opens its record. To play a mission again, start a fresh campaign with the same or a different seed.
- The next mission uses the same campaign seed and the confirmed outcomes of earlier missions. Show consequential changes in the opening briefing so the player can connect them to earlier choices. For example, delayed communications reduce the initial reliability of flight reports. Flight disruptions increase simulated withdrawal demand. If the bank mission comes first, broad holds delay simulated staffing or service payments in a subsequent mission. Apply each preceding consequence once and cap its effect so every mission remains playable.
- Every mission contains a clue about D-83. The combined timeline reveals how one exercise generated the alerts Joshua counted separately. Preserve the fictional incident chronology across all six mission orders and keep it distinct from the player's real-time deadline. Keep the underlying records in the finale even after a failed mission. Highlight records the player inspected during play and preserve independently checked observations in the case file.
- Once all three debriefs are acknowledged, add a fourth selectable row, 4. GLOBAL FAILSAFE. Unlock it even if some missions went badly. Better decisions preserve more options in the finale. The end credits or debrief let the player start a fresh campaign with the same or a different seed.
