Glass TTY VT220
===============

Designer: Viacheslav Slavinsky (svofski)
Status: Public domain.

A pixel-accurate revival of the DEC VT220 terminal typeface. Released by the
author into the public domain for any use.

Source TTF: https://github.com/svofski/glasstty (Glass_TTY_VT220.ttf)
Converted locally to WOFF2 (glass-tty-vt220.woff2) for self-hosting. No
runtime network request is made for this font; it ships with the build.
